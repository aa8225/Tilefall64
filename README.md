# Tilefall 64

An 8-bit tile-catching arcade game by **AA Software Ltd**.

**Play it:** https://aa8225.github.io/tilefall64/

Catch tumbling tiles on your paddle, drop them into the bin, and line up three of a colour across, down or diagonally. Flashing rainbow tiles are wild: they complete a line of three in any colour, or clear a whole column. Eighteen scenes that journey from the first sunrise to a pale blue dot in space, each with its own thought; changing wave goals including bonus rounds, survival and clear-the-bin; an original chiptune soundtrack and a saved top-10 high score table.

Works in any modern phone or computer browser. Tap **SHARE** in the game for a QR code, a link to send, and one-tap install to the home screen. Installed copies update automatically and play offline.
